# The paper bench is mixing blades and adjustment ends

After a cutting unit comes off the grinder, our shop proof requires both paper checks at the five posted bedknife marks for every blade. Paper drawn lengthwise passes only with light, even drag; moving freely or binding does not pass. Paper presented upright passes only with a clean cut; a ragged, partial, or missed cut does not pass. An observation belongs only to its blade, mark, and paper direction, and it should still be there when I rotate away and return. Recording two checks quickly must keep both, and a late result from a plate I just left must not replace the new plate.

The fixture plate is authoritative for blade count, which screen side is the drive end, each adjuster’s movement per effective click, closing direction, safe handle-click limit, last approach direction, and reversal take-up, plus the correction requested by No drag or Binding. A take-up click is needed only when the recommended turn reverses that end’s last approach; it counts against the handle-click limit but adds no bedbar movement. Every blade at a mark matters, and one poor result cannot be averaged away. The bedbar moves as a straight line between its two adjusters: the drive-quarter mark receives three quarters of drive-end movement and one quarter of non-drive movement, center receives half of each, and the non-drive quarter reverses those shares.

Recommend the effective whole-click pair with the fewest total handle clicks, including any take-up, that supplies every observed lengthwise correction through that straight-line movement. When equally small pairs work, prefer the pair with the smaller largest handle movement, then the one assigning more of the tie to the drive end. Translate each signed movement through that end’s posted opening or closing direction; show total handle clicks, but show movement from effective clicks only. If every request points the same way but the posted limits cannot supply enough movement, show the closest capped pair and make each reached limit clear. If opening and closing requests have no safe pair inside the limits, withhold both recommendations.

Our edge-pattern safeguard also matters after grinding. When one blade asks for the same opening or closing correction at three or more marks while another tested blade passes those marks, identify that blade for another edge check and withhold both bedbar turns. The same correction appearing at one mark across several blades is a bedbar result, not a blade-edge pattern.

Only lengthwise drag contributes to bedbar movement; upright cut quality still blocks release. Switching millimetres and inches is presentation only and must not change observations, clicks, directions, limits, or release readiness. RELEASE stays unavailable until the complete blade-by-mark-by-direction proof passes and I confirm one full hand rotation with no hard spot.
