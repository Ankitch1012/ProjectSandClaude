# My chair survey is attaching notes to the wrong spring cords

I’m recording the exposed foundation on CHAIR-41 before our conservator decides on treatment. Choosing a cord from the strip can open a different run, and pressing the same cord in the drawing can select another run after I resize the page. The FRONT and CHAIR LEFT marks, and the direction names based on them, should follow each record’s chair orientation even when a cord is drawn at an angle. I need the printed cord tag to follow the same anchored run whichever end I read it from, and the two clear findings already on this chair should not disappear from the register.

The loose crossings in this chair are not knots. The one marked lashing shows contact between two cords, but it still does not turn them into one cord. For my eight-way survey, a coil is fully mapped only when four different frame-to-frame cords give it opposite contacts in the crosswise, lengthwise, rising-diagonal, and falling-diagonal directions. Extra spans or repeated contacts from one cord should not inflate the register’s cord count or make an incomplete coil look complete, and tacks on the chamfered rail are real anchors even when two cords share one.

When I mark a damaged span, I expect the rest of that cord to be marked without changing a cord that merely crosses it. A replacement that only matches in height, or differs in diameter, wire gauge, or turn count, still needs review, and proposing one means its old contact checks need doing again. Failed webbing should stay visible and send the record for conservator review instead of hiding the supported coils.

Completion is only about having the underside documented; it does not mean treatment is approved. CHAIR-63 uses its posted one-course front support, while SETTEE-08 uses its recorded front-wire continuity. I need the survey to show completion and conservator review separately, without making any claim that the chair is safe to cut, repair, or return to use.
