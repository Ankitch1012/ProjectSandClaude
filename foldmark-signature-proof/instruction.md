# Keep the press-sheet proof tied to the revision I release

When I prepare the sample booklet, its signature cannot be trusted as a physical proof: the page positions on the front and back do not always survive folding as shown, and regenerating after a paper, binding, orientation, fold, bleed, or page-assignment change can leave parts of the proof behind. A signature must expose the actual sheet positions, keep each duplex pair together, contain exactly the pages its binding supports, and recalculate every page region and sheet-level mark from the current controls.

Saving must preserve exactly the proof on screen. If another session saves first, stale work must be rejected instead of replacing it; changes made in the same proof cycle keep the order I applied them. Validation and production release must belong to the currently displayed saved revision, so an incomplete, changed, stale, or previously validated proof cannot authorize the revision I am viewing.

At both the supported 1280 × 900 desktop view and a 760-pixel-wide view, I need the sheets, page labels, setup controls, validation status, and save and release actions to remain readable and reachable without covering one another.
