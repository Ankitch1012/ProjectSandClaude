# My chair survey is attaching notes to the wrong spring cords

I’m recording the exposed foundation on CHAIR-41 before our conservator decides on treatment. Choosing a cord from the strip can open a different run, and pressing the same cord in the drawing can select another run after I resize the page. The FRONT and CHAIR LEFT marks, and the direction names based on them, should follow each record’s chair orientation even when a cord is drawn at an angle. I need the printed cord tag to follow the same anchored run whichever end I read it from, and the two clear findings already on this chair should not disappear from the register.

The loose crossings in this chair are not knots. The one marked lashing shows contact between two cords, but it still does not turn them into one cord. For my eight-way survey, a coil is fully mapped only when its anchored cord contacts cover four different directions: crosswise, lengthwise, rising diagonal, and falling diagonal. Repeating one direction should not make an incomplete coil look complete, extra drawn spans should not inflate the physical cord count, and tacks on the chamfered rail are real anchors.

When I mark a damaged span, I expect that finding to stay with the complete cord without changing a cord at either a loose crossing or the marked lashing. A replacement that differs in wire gauge, turn count, or either recorded diameter still needs review, while Profile A remains a match; proposing any profile means its old contact checks need doing again. Failed webbing should stay visible and send the record for conservator review instead of hiding the supported coils.

Completion is only about having the underside documented. CHAIR-63 uses its posted one-course front support, while SETTEE-08 uses its recorded front-wire continuity. I need the survey to show completion and conservator review separately; I use both as documentation for our conservator, not as treatment or safety approval.
